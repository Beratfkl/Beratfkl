### About Me

I am a Computer Engineering student based in Eskişehir, Turkey. 

I prefer building software from first principles rather than stitching together black-box game engines or heavy frameworks. Most of my recent work focuses on pure Java, real-time simulation loops, and mathematical models (trigonometric projections, custom rendering pipelines, and coordinate systems). I also enjoy writing language tooling, specifically recursive-descent parsers and AST interpreters.

When I write code, I prioritize strict separation of concerns, explicit thread-safety, and keeping things readable without unnecessary layers of abstraction.
